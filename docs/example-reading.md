# Synthetic example / 合成示例

This example is invented for documentation and matches the built-in demonstration. It is not an actual employer posting or an evaluated research result.

## Source / 原文

**示例 · 数据分析实习生**

岗位职责：使用SQL核对订单与退款，独立完成每周经营分析报表，与财务团队核对指标口径。任职要求：逻辑清晰，能说明判断依据，形成可追溯的业务改进建议。

**Example · Data Analyst Intern**

Responsibilities: use SQL to reconcile orders and refunds; independently prepare weekly business analysis reports; and verify metric definitions with the finance team. Requirements: clear logical reasoning, the ability to explain the evidence behind judgments, and traceable recommendations for business improvement.

## Reading / 分析

**SQL：这里首先是核对能力。** 原文把SQL连到“订单与退款”，应先看查询、对账和异常定位；不能据此增加模型开发或数据架构职责。可展示的成果是带核对依据的报表或异常清单，属于分析建议的产出示例。

**“独立”约束交付，“共同核对”约束口径。** “独立完成每周经营分析报表”并不意味着一个人有权定义指标；同段还要求“与财务团队核对指标口径”。两者可以构成有组织的专业分工。

**社会学判断：** 文本将成果的个人归属与判断依据的共同确认放在一起。独立在这里是对交付主体的安排，尚不能直接读成决策自治或支持不足。一个需要确认的分歧是：当业务与财务的口径不一致，谁能裁定，以及报表期限是否允许等待裁定？这会改变我们对该岗位协调负担的判断。

**SQL is first a reconciliation skill here.** The source connects it to orders and refunds. Queries, reconciliation and anomaly investigation are plausible task-level readings; model development and data architecture are not stated duties. An evidence-linked report or exception list would be an illustrative output, not an employer-specified deliverable.

**Independence governs delivery; joint checking governs definitions.** Producing the weekly report independently does not establish sole authority over metrics. The same passage requires checking definitions with finance.

**Sociological reading:** the text assigns individual ownership of an output while locating its evidential basis in a joint process. This is a division of work; it need not mean decision autonomy or absent support. Ask who resolves a disagreement over definitions and whether the deadline accommodates that resolution. The answer changes the interpretation of coordination work.
