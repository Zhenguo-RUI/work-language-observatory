# Method and review / 分析与评价

The unit of analysis is a **term in a situated passage**, not a personality label detached from its job. The output should tell the reader what a phrase asks someone to do, what it does not establish, and what evidence would change the interpretation.

## Reading sequence

1. Preserve the original title, JD, source and required/preferred/negative modalities. Separate employer duties from intermediary promotional text.
2. Relate a term to nearby tasks, objects, outputs, collaborators, stated resources and decision rights. Do not convert co-occurrence into a causal fact.
3. Offer a concise interpretation and a plausible alternative. “Not stated” is a disclosure observation, not proof that a right or resource does not exist.
4. Explain a consequential organizational relation: for example, independent delivery can coexist with joint definition of measurement. Do not manufacture a tension just to sound critical.
5. Ask questions whose answers could change the reading, and preserve hypothetical answer branches as hypotheses rather than employer facts.
6. Render the analysis in Chinese and English; retain source wording and a separately labelled full translation.

The approach draws on the contextual study of keywords, professional jurisdiction, labour process and organization, skill combinations and corpus provenance. These are analytical resources, not a theory detector that can prove a mechanism from a word. A JD presents expectations and promises; it does not by itself document lived work. Their textual modalities can differ without either being treated as verified practice.

## Six review dimensions

Scores use a 0–4 scale: 0 unusable, 1 major errors, 2 mixed, 3 good with limited corrections, 4 strong and evidence-led. Weights specify a review rubric, not measured performance.

| Dimension | Weight | Reviewer asks |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Source fidelity / 原文准确性 | 25% | Are duties, authority, numbers and modalities preserved? Are quotes exact? |
| Situated differentiation / 语境辨析 | 20% | Is this term explained through this role's tasks rather than a generic dictionary meaning? |
| Practical usefulness / 可用性 | 15% | Can a reader identify likely work outputs and ask a consequential question? |
| Sociological explanation / 社会学解释 | 20% | Does a supported relation explain how work is organized, with a serious alternative? |
| Provenance and limits / 来源与边界 | 10% | Are source facts, contextual references and hypotheses distinguishable? |
| Bilingual clarity / 双语与可读性 | 10% | Are titles, full source translation and analysis complete, concise and faithful? |

A fabricated quote, invented duty or reversal of a mandatory requirement blocks release regardless of the weighted score. A schema pass cannot compensate for a substantive error. Model self-review is a diagnostic aid, not independent expert validation.

For a comparison of prompt versions, freeze the inputs and record the versions, outputs, reviewer identity/type, dimension scores and specific failure causes. Keep development cases separate from a held-out test set. Only claim improvement after a comparable assessment, reporting unresolved failures as well as successes.

## 中文说明

分析要在两件事之间保持张力：不能把“抗压”直接判成剥削，也不能把逐字摘录当成分析。理论判断必须说清楚：哪几处措辞构成什么关系、为什么这个解释有意义、还有什么解释成立，以及哪种新证据会改变判断。

公开代码的自动检查只测试有限的结构与已知错误。评测有效性仍需要独立的人类复核、明确分母与案例级证据。
