# 可以引用的文献

## 使用规则

- 只引用这里的条目，只用来支持"用途"写明的内容。
- 页面正文第一次引用写作者和年份，参考文献一节给完整信息和链接。参考文献只列正文用到的。
- 想加新文献：先打开 DOI 或出版社页面，核对作者、年份、题目、刊名和卷期，再按同样格式加到这里，并告诉用户加了什么。核对不了的不加。
- 不要为了显得全面而堆文献。一处论点配一篇最直接的。

## 问题与理论

- Urciuoli, Bonnie. 2008. "Skills and Selves in the New Workplace." *American Ethnologist* 35(2): 211–228. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1548-1425.2008.00031.x
  用途：技能话语把工作关系写成可评估的个人品质。研究问题的出发点。
- Williams, Raymond. 1976（1983 年修订版）. *Keywords: A Vocabulary of Culture and Society*. London: Fontana.
  用途：关键词研究的传统，一个词的分歧用法本身就是研究对象。词典页的理论来源。
- Leary, John Patrick. 2018. *Keywords: The New Language of Capitalism*. Chicago: Haymarket Books.
  用途：用关键词的方法读当代职场用语。讨论"抗压"这类词时可以对照。
- Abbott, Andrew. 1988. *The System of Professions: An Essay on the Division of Expert Labor*. Chicago: University of Chicago Press.
  用途：区分专业判断、分工和管辖权。讨论"能做"和"能决定"时用。
- Bowker, Geoffrey C., and Susan Leigh Star. 1999. *Sorting Things Out: Classification and Its Consequences*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.
  用途：分类是有后果的选择。说明编码手册、词表分组和职业分类都是作者的解释时用。
- Drucker, Johanna. 2011. "Humanities Approaches to Graphical Display." *Digital Humanities Quarterly* 5(1). https://dhq-static.digitalhumanities.org/pdf/000091.pdf
  用途：图表里的类别带着解释选择。写图表说明时用。

## 招聘广告作为数据

- Deming, David, and Lisa B. Kahn. 2018. "Skill Requirements across Firms and Labor Markets: Evidence from Job Postings for Professionals." *Journal of Labor Economics* 36(S1): S337–S369. https://doi.org/10.1086/694106
  用途：用关键词把招聘广告里的技能要求当作需求数据。本研究与它对照：不只数词，还看词在语境里描述什么。
- Kuhn, Peter, and Kailing Shen. 2013. "Gender Discrimination in Job Ads: Evidence from China." *Quarterly Journal of Economics* 128(1): 287–336. https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjs046
  用途：中文招聘网站文本的代表性研究，说明中文招聘广告可以作为研究数据。
- Stephany, Fabian, and Ole Teutloff. 2024. "What Is the Price of a Skill? The Value of Complementarity." *Research Policy* 53(1). 预印本：https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01535
  用途：技能组合的价值。说明本研究不把词面同现写成技能互补或工资溢价。

## 文本分析方法

- Grimmer, Justin, Margaret E. Roberts, and Brandon M. Stewart. 2022. *Text as Data: A New Framework for Machine Learning and the Social Sciences*. Princeton: Princeton University Press.
  用途：文本测量要针对研究问题来验证。整体方法框架。
- Grimmer, Justin, and Brandon M. Stewart. 2013. "Text as Data: The Promise and Pitfalls of Automatic Content Analysis Methods for Political Texts." *Political Analysis* 21(3): 267–297. https://doi.org/10.1093/pan/mps028
  用途：自动方法不能替代细读，必须验证。
- Nelson, Laura K. 2020. "Computational Grounded Theory: A Methodological Framework." *Sociological Methods & Research* 49(1): 3–42. https://doi.org/10.1177/0049124117729703
  用途：找模式、细读修正、确认三步。整个研究流程的组织方式。
- Monroe, Burt L., Michael P. Colaresi, and Kevin M. Quinn. 2008. "Fightin' Words: Lexical Feature Selection and Evaluation for Identifying the Content of Political Conflict." *Political Analysis* 16(4): 372–403. https://doi.org/10.1093/pan/mpn018
  用途：带先验的 log-odds 比较两组用词。搭配词比较。
- Rodriguez, Pedro L., Arthur Spirling, and Brandon M. Stewart. 2023. "Embedding Regression: Models for Context-Specific Description and Inference." *American Political Science Review* 117(4): 1255–1274. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0003055422001228
  用途：比较同一个词在不同群体里的语境含义。只在做了方法卡第 10 节时引用。
- Smith, David A., Ryan Cordell, and Abby Mullen. 2015. "Computational Methods for Uncovering Reprinted Texts in Antebellum Newspapers." *American Literary History* 27(3). https://muse.jhu.edu/article/687864
  用途：数字人文里的文本复用检测。模板句检测。

## 模型标注与验证

- Egami, Naoki, Musashi Hinck, Brandon M. Stewart, and Hanying Wei. 2023. "Using Imperfect Surrogates for Downstream Inference: Design-based Supervised Learning for Social Science Applications of Large Language Models." *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems* 36. https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/d862f7f5445255090de13b825b880d59-Abstract.html
  用途：用模型标签加随机人工标签做无偏估计（DSL）。
- Gilardi, Fabrizio, Meysam Alizadeh, and Maël Kubli. 2023. "ChatGPT Outperforms Crowd Workers for Text-Annotation Tasks." *PNAS* 120(30): e2305016120. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2305016120
  用途：模型标注值得一试。不能用来证明本项目的模型标注准确。
- Törnberg, Petter. 2024. "Best Practices for Text Annotation with Large Language Models." *Sociologica* 18(2). https://sociologica.unibo.it/article/view/19461
  用途：模型标注的操作规范，包括提示词版本、验证和透明报告。
- Krippendorff, Klaus. 2018. *Content Analysis: An Introduction to Its Methodology*. 4th ed. Thousand Oaks, CA: SAGE.
  用途：α 的计算方法和阈值。

## AI 与数据劳动（写"闭环"相关发现时用）

- Tubaro, Paola, Antonio A. Casilli, and Marion Coville. 2020. "The Trainer, the Verifier, the Imitator: Three Ways in Which Human Platform Workers Support Artificial Intelligence." *Big Data & Society* 7(1). https://doi.org/10.1177/2053951720919776
  用途：人在 AI 生产中承担训练、核验等工作。讨论"把现场问题整理成模型需求"这类工作时用。
- Gray, Mary L., and Siddharth Suri. 2019. *Ghost Work: How to Stop Silicon Valley from Building a New Global Underclass*. Boston: Houghton Mifflin Harcourt.
  用途：同上，作背景补充。
